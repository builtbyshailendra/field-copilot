# MP-114: MRI of the lumbar spine (CPT 72148, 72149, 72158)

Synthetic policy for the fictional Tallowmere Health Plan. Not clinical guidance.

## Criteria
An MRI of the lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when **one** of the following applies:

### A. Red-flag symptoms
Any of: new bowel or bladder dysfunction, progressive motor weakness, suspected infection (fever with back pain in an immunocompromised member), known cancer with new back pain, or significant trauma. No period of conservative therapy is required for red flags.

### B. Persistent radiculopathy
Back pain with leg symptoms in a nerve-root pattern that has lasted at least **6 weeks**, **and** at least **6 weeks of conservative therapy** has been tried and failed. Conservative therapy means physical therapy, a home exercise program supervised by a clinician, or both, together with medication as tolerated.

### C. Surgical or injection planning
The member is being evaluated for spine surgery or an epidural steroid injection and the treating surgeon or pain specialist has documented the plan.

## Not medically necessary
- Back pain under 6 weeks without red flags.
- Repeat imaging within 12 months without a change in symptoms or a planned procedure.

## Required documentation
Clinical notes describing the symptoms and their duration, the conservative therapy tried with dates, and any red-flag findings.
