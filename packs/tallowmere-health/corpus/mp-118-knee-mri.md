# MP-118: MRI of the knee (CPT 73721)

Synthetic policy for the fictional Tallowmere Health Plan. Not clinical guidance.

## Criteria
An MRI of the knee is considered medically necessary when the member has had **plain X-rays of the knee within the last 60 days** and **one** of the following:

### A. Suspected internal derangement
Mechanical symptoms (locking, catching, giving way) or a positive meniscal or ligament test on examination, after an injury.

### B. Persistent pain after conservative care
Knee pain lasting at least **4 weeks** despite conservative care: rest, anti-inflammatory medication as tolerated, and physical therapy or a home exercise program.

### C. Surgical planning
An orthopedic surgeon has documented that the result will change the surgical plan.

## Exceptions to the X-ray requirement
X-rays are not required for a suspected acute ligament tear in a member under 18, or when the X-ray was done at an urgent care visit and the report is attached.

## Not medically necessary
- Knee pain of under 4 weeks without mechanical symptoms or injury.
- Imaging requested only because the member asked for it.
