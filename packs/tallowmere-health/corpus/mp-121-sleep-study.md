# MP-121: Sleep studies (CPT 95810, 95811, G0399)

Synthetic policy for the fictional Tallowmere Health Plan. Not clinical guidance.

## Home sleep apnea test first
For suspected obstructive sleep apnea in an adult without significant other conditions, a **home sleep apnea test (HSAT)** is the first test. An in-lab polysomnography is considered medically necessary only when:
- the HSAT was negative or technically inadequate and suspicion remains, or
- the member has a condition that makes an HSAT unreliable: moderate to severe lung disease, neuromuscular disease, heart failure, or chronic opioid use.

## Clinical criteria for any sleep study
Documented symptoms such as loud snoring, witnessed pauses in breathing, or daytime sleepiness, with an **Epworth Sleepiness Scale score of 10 or more**, or a STOP-BANG score of 3 or more.

## Required documentation
- The sleep questionnaire score with its date.
- **BMI** recorded within the last 6 months.
- For in-lab studies: the HSAT report or the documented reason an HSAT is unreliable.
- A **sleep diary** covering at least 7 days when insomnia is part of the complaint.

## Repeat studies
A repeat study within 12 months requires a documented change: weight change of 10% or more, new symptoms, or titration after surgery.
