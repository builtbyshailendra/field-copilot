# MP-130: Bariatric surgery (CPT 43644, 43775)

Synthetic policy for the fictional Tallowmere Health Plan. Not clinical guidance.

## Criteria
Bariatric surgery for adults (18 or older) is considered medically necessary when all of the following are documented:
1. **BMI of 40 or more**, or **BMI of 35 or more with at least one obesity-related condition** (type 2 diabetes, obstructive sleep apnea, hypertension resistant to treatment).
2. Participation in a **supervised weight-management program for at least 3 consecutive months** within the last 2 years, with monthly visits recorded.
3. A **psychological evaluation** within the last 12 months that finds no condition that would prevent safe surgery and recovery.
4. A **nutrition consultation** with a registered dietitian.

## Adolescents
Members aged 13 to 17 are reviewed case by case by a medical director.

## Required documentation
Weight and BMI history for the last 2 years, program visit notes, the psychological evaluation report, and the dietitian's note.

## Revision surgery
A revision is reviewed by a medical director and needs documentation of the complication or the failure of the first procedure.
