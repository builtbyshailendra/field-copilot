# MP-142: Outpatient physical therapy (CPT 97110, 97140, 97161-97163)

Synthetic policy for the fictional Tallowmere Health Plan. Not clinical guidance.

## Initial visits
The first **12 visits** per condition per calendar year do not need prior authorization, provided an evaluation (97161-97163) is on file.

## Additional visits
Visits beyond 12 need prior authorization and are considered medically necessary when:
- the therapist documents **measurable functional goals** and **progress toward them** (for example range of motion in degrees or distance walked), and
- the member is expected to keep improving, not just maintain.

Up to **8 more visits** may be authorized at a time.

## Maintenance therapy
Therapy only to maintain function, with no expected improvement, is not medically necessary under this policy, except for members with a progressive neurological condition reviewed by a medical director.

## Required documentation
The initial evaluation, the most recent progress note with measurements, and the updated plan of care with the number of visits requested.
