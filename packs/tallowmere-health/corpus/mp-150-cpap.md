# MP-150: CPAP devices (HCPCS E0601)

Synthetic policy for the fictional Tallowmere Health Plan. Not clinical guidance.

## Initial 90-day rental
A CPAP device is covered for an initial **90-day rental** when a sleep study meeting MP-121 shows an **apnea-hypopnea index (AHI) of 15 or more**, or an AHI of 5 to 14 with documented daytime sleepiness, hypertension, or a history of stroke.

## Continued coverage after 90 days
Coverage continues when the device's download shows **use of 4 hours or more per night on at least 70% of nights** during a consecutive 30-day period in the first 90 days, and a clinician documents that symptoms improved.

## Replacement
A replacement device is covered after 5 years, or sooner if the device is damaged beyond repair (not by misuse).

## Required documentation
The sleep study report with the AHI, the prescribing clinician's order, and for continued coverage the 30-day usage download.
