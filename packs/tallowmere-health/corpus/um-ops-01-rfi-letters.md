# UM-OPS-01: Request-for-information (RFI) letters

## When to send an RFI
Send an RFI when a request is missing documentation the policy lists as required. Ask for **everything missing in one letter**. Sending several letters one item at a time delays the decision and is the most common complaint from provider offices.

## What every RFI must contain
1. The request ID and the service requested (code and plain description).
2. A numbered list of each missing item, with the policy number that requires it.
3. The **response deadline**: 14 calendar days from the letter date for standard requests, 2 business days for expedited requests.
4. How to send documents: the provider portal, fax 1-555-0142, or electronic attachment (X12 275).
5. The reviewing nurse's name and a callback number.

## Tone
Plain, specific and polite. No clinical opinions about whether the request will be approved. Never state or imply a decision in an RFI.

## Approval and sending
An RFI drafted by any tool is a draft until the reviewing nurse approves it. The nurse sends it from the review system; the send is logged on the case.
