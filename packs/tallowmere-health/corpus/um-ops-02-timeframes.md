# UM-OPS-02: Decision timeframes

## Deadlines (from CMS-0057-F, as applied by Tallowmere)
- **Expedited requests:** a decision within **72 hours** of receipt. A request is expedited when the requesting clinician indicates that waiting could seriously jeopardize the member's life, health or ability to regain maximum function.
- **Standard requests:** a decision within **7 calendar days** of receipt.

## When the clock starts and pauses
The clock starts when the request is received through any channel. Sending an RFI does not stop the clock for Medicare Advantage members; for other lines of business the clock pauses while waiting for the provider's response, as allowed by the member's plan type.

## Denials
Only a medical director can deny a request for medical necessity. Every denial gives a specific reason that names the policy criterion not met.

## Reporting
Decision times, approval rates and denial reasons are reported publicly each year as required.
